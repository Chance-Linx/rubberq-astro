# RubberQ Production Process & Quality Control (IATF 16949 Compliant)

This document maps the core manufacturing capabilities of RubberQ to the rigorous requirements of the automotive industry.

## 1. Full-Stack Manufacturing Flow

### Stage A: Material R&D & Compounding
- **Process**: Formulation Design → Raw Material Inspection → Automatic Weighing → Internal Mixing (Banbury Mixer) → Open Mill Sheeting.
- **Key Equipment**: High-end Internal Mixers, Automatic Ingredient Scales.
- **QC Node**: Rheometer Testing (T90, T10, ML, MH) for every batch.

### Stage B: Pre-forming & Preparation
- **Process**: Extrusion / Pre-forming → Cutting → Barcoding.
- **QC Node**: Weight consistency check, Barcode traceability initialization.

### Stage C: Vulcanization (Molding)
- **Process**: Compression Molding / Injection Molding / Transfer Molding.
- **Key Equipment**: Multi-tonnage Vacuum Vulcanizing Presses.
- **QC Node**: Real-time over-temperature alarm systems, Curing time monitoring.

### Stage D: Post-Processing & Finishing
- **Process**: Deflashing (Cryogenic or Manual) → Post-Curing (if required for FKM/Silicone).
- **QC Node**: Visual inspection for flash/defects.

### Stage E: Final Inspection & Testing
- **Process**: 100% Visual Inspection or Automated CCD Inspection.
- **Laboratory Tests**: Tensile Strength, Elongation at Break, Hardness (Shore A), Heat Aging, Oil Immersion, Compression Set.

## 2. IATF 16949:2016 Integration
RubberQ maintains the following automotive-grade controls:
- **APQP (Advanced Product Quality Planning)**: Collaboration from the prototype stage.
- **PPAP (Production Part Approval Process)**: Full documentation for OE customers.
- **FMEA (Failure Mode and Effects Analysis)**: Risk mitigation for design and process.
- **SPC (Statistical Process Control)**: Monitoring critical dimensions via ERP.
- **MSA (Measurement Systems Analysis)**: Ensuring gauge reliability.

## 3. Traceability System
- **ERP Integration**: All production records from raw material batch to final shipment are logged.
- **Barcoding**: Each carton/lot is traceable back to the operator, machine, and material batch.
