How to specify an AS568 O-ring

AS568 is the standard list of inch O-ring sizes used across the United States. It is published by SAE International. Each size has a dash number, such as -214, that fixes the inside diameter, the cross-section and their tolerances.

AS568 does not set the material or the hardness. Give the dash number, the material and the hardness, for example: AS568-214, Nitrile (NBR), 70 Shore A. A 70 durometer nitrile is a common general-purpose choice for oil and hydraulic service. For other fluids and temperatures, see the material pages.

Questions we hear

What does the first digit of an AS568 dash number mean?
It gives the cross-section series: 0 for 1/16 in. (-004 to -050), 1 for 3/32 in., 2 for 1/8 in., 3 for 3/16 in. and 4 for 1/4 in. The -9xx sizes are for straight-thread tube fitting bosses.
Does AS568 specify the O-ring material?
No. AS568 covers sizes and tolerances only. Name the material and hardness separately, or send the full spec from your drawing.
What if the size I need is not in AS568?
Send the inside diameter and cross-section you need, or a drawing or sample. We make non-standard O-rings, including large spliced rings.
