# Custom Rubber Molding

Review a molding route for your part, compound and order requirements. Tooling, dimensional priorities, parting lines and inspection need to be considered together.

## Design for a repeatable part

Mark critical dimensions and functional surfaces. Discuss draft, wall transitions, insert position, flash and the likely parting line.

## Compare process options

Compression, transfer and injection routes have different tooling and material considerations. We review project fit and available production arrangements for the actual part.

## Approve the sample basis

Agree the drawing revision, inspection plan and functional checks. Include forecast demand and requirements for later changes.

## Review a Molding Project

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
