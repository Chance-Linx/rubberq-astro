# AEM Material Selection for Molded Parts

Discuss AEM candidates for a defined component and service environment. The required properties, geometry and validation conditions guide the formulation and manufacturing review.

## Share the operating profile

Include normal and peak temperatures, fluid exposure, motion and the expected deformation of the part.

## Make acceptance measurable

Specify hardness, relevant physical properties and the conditioning methods used to assess a candidate compound.

## Review manufacturing and fit

Send the drawing and critical interfaces. Discuss sample inspection and the functional checks needed before a production decision.

## Review a AEM Application

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
