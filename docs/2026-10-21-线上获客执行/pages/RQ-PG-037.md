## 英文页面稿

# Rubber Standards and Specification Support

Use standards to make requirements measurable. Identify the applicable edition, test conditions and acceptance criteria alongside your material or part drawing.

## Material classification

Discuss the callout, suffix requirements and intended service conditions when using a material classification such as ASTM D2000.

## Part dimensions and tolerances

Specify the reference system, drawing limits and inspection method. O-ring size references and molded-part tolerances serve different purposes.

## Testing and reporting

Include the method and conditioning with each requested result. A standard number alone does not define every project acceptance requirement.

## Discuss Your Project

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
