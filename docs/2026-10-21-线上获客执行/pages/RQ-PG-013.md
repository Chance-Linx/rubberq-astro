# FKM Materials for Industrial Seals

Review FKM compounds for a specified fluid and temperature environment. Chemical compatibility, low-temperature behaviour and compression recovery depend on the formulation and application.

## Describe every fluid

Include fuel, oil, additives, cleaning solutions and mixtures. Material family names alone do not establish suitability for the full exposure sequence.

## Review temperature and movement

Provide continuous and peak temperatures, cold-start conditions, pressure and seal motion. Include the expected compression and installation requirements.

## Specify and validate

Agree the compound specification and a representative sample test before approving repeat supply. If an existing designation is referenced, include its exact grade.

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
