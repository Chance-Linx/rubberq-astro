# Silicone Materials for Custom Molded Parts

Discuss silicone candidates for your component geometry and operating conditions. Flexibility, temperature exposure, contact media and assembly requirements guide the review.

## Choose a route for the geometry

Share wall sections, thin features, inserts and dimensional priorities. The material and molding process need to suit the same part.

## Review the full environment

Include fluids, cleaning conditions, temperature cycles, mechanical loads and any electrical requirements with their test methods.

## Agree finish and inspection

Specify colour, appearance, flash limits and the critical dimensions. Application-specific regulatory or certification requirements need separate review.

## Review a Silicone Application

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
