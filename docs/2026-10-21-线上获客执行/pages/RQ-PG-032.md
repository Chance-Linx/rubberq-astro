# Rubber Components for EV Thermal Management

Review a rubber component for the fluid, temperature cycle and assembly conditions of your thermal-management project. Bring the drawing and specification to define the material and manufacturing discussion.

## Specify the exposure

Identify the service fluid and additives, temperature range, cycling profile and any cleaning exposure. Include electrical requirements only with the applicable test criteria.

## Review component interfaces

Share the mating geometry, tolerances, compression and installation method. Part-level requirements determine the validation needed.

## Prepare supplier documentation

Discuss traceability, inspection plans and customer-required documentation. Sample approval and production commitments follow the technical review.

## Discuss Your Application

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
