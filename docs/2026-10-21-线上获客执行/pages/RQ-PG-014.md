## 英文页面稿

# EPDM Materials for Seals and Gaskets

Discuss EPDM candidates for the weather, water or formulated-fluid exposure in your assembly. Selection should account for the complete medium, temperature cycle and mechanical requirements.

## Provide the fluid formulation

For coolant-contact projects, identify the full mixture and additives rather than only the base fluid. Cleaning chemicals and contaminants also belong in the review.

## Review the compressed assembly

Describe squeeze, joint movement, flange condition and temperature cycling. Discuss how the seal will be installed and retained.

## Agree a compatibility test

Define immersion conditions, relevant property changes and assembly checks for the selected compound. Published family comparisons are only a starting point.

## Review a EPDM Application

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
