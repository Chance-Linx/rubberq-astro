# FFKM Replacement Seals for Equipment Maintenance

Assess an FFKM replacement around the seal specification and equipment conditions. Share the existing part reference, dimensions, service chemistry and failure history for a technical review.

## Bring the original requirements

Include the current seal drawing or dimensions, material designation, equipment position and service history. A branded part reference is a starting point for discussion, not proof of interchangeability.

## Compare what the application needs

Review temperature cycles, process media, vacuum conditions, installation and the relevant physical-property requirements. Additional purity, outgassing or process-specific tests are discussed for the project.

## Validate before changing supply

Agree sample checks and equipment qualification with your engineering team. Material selection does not by itself establish equivalent life, plasma performance or OEM approval.

## Questions about your project

### Do you guarantee a drop-in replacement?

Fit and performance must be established for the application. We review the specification and agree the validation needed before a supply change.

### Can I send a Kalrez part reference?

You can include an existing brand and part reference with your specification. Brand references identify the part being reviewed and do not imply affiliation or an approved equivalent.

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
