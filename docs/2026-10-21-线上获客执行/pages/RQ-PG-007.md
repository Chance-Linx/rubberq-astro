## 英文页面稿

# Custom Molded Rubber Parts

Turn a drawing or functional requirement into a rubber component development discussion. RubberQ reviews compound selection, geometry, tooling and inspection as a connected manufacturing task.

## Make the drawing work for rubber

Identify critical dimensions and assembly features. Review parting lines, flash limits, draft, wall sections and features that may be affected by molding and shrinkage.

## Choose the process around the part

Part geometry, compound, insert requirements and order volume guide the molding route. Tooling and sampling requirements are discussed after the technical review.

## Prepare for repeat production

Include drawing revision, forecast demand, inspection requirements and packaging. Agree how approved samples, compound identification and production records will be maintained.

## Send a Custom Part RFQ

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
