# Liquid Silicone Rubber Materials and Part Review

Review liquid silicone rubber as a candidate route for your drawing. Part geometry, material grade, tooling and inspection requirements determine the project discussion.

## Review the part and tool together

Provide a drawing showing thin features, sealing edges, parting-line restrictions and inserts. Include expected order volume and the critical assembly dimensions.

## Specify the grade and exposure

Describe hardness, colour, temperature and fluid exposure. Any certification requirement must identify the actual grade and intended application.

## Define the sampling route

Discuss tooling, sample inspection and functional checks before agreeing production. Project-specific process availability is confirmed during the manufacturing review.

## Review a LSR Application

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
