## 英文页面稿

# Rubber Seals for Semiconductor Equipment

Review an equipment seal against its actual process conditions. FFKM and FKM candidates require a defined specification, application review and qualification plan.

## Map the seal environment

Identify the seal location, process chemistry, temperature cycle and vacuum requirements. Include cleaning and maintenance conditions and any contamination limits.

## Separate material and equipment qualification

A material designation does not establish plasma resistance, cleanliness or seal life. Discuss the compound-specific data and tests relevant to your process.

## Support maintenance and development

Bring the drawing or existing part specification for sample review. Equipment maintenance replacements and new developments need an agreed approval route.

## Discuss Your Application

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
