## 英文页面稿

# Rubber Components for Liquid Cooling and Energy Storage

Discuss sealing components against the fluid, joint and environmental conditions of your cooling or energy-storage equipment. Material selection and assembly validation are reviewed together.

## Identify the fluid-contact requirement

Provide the full fluid formulation, additives, temperature and pressure cycles. Include cleaning exposure and the material interfaces in contact with the seal.

## Review the joint and enclosure separately

Fluid-contact seals and enclosure gaskets perform different jobs. Share the groove, clamp or flange design and the compression and movement requirements.

## Define the validation route

Discuss immersion tests, dimensional changes, compression recovery and assembled checks. Electrical or flame requirements must identify the actual method and compound being assessed.
