## 英文页面稿

# Rubber-to-Metal Bonded Components

Review bonded rubber components with attention to the elastomer, metal insert and service load. Share the assembly drawing and loading conditions to discuss a development and inspection route.

## Understand the load path

Describe compression, shear, tension and repeated movement. Mark the bonded surfaces and the functional direction of loading on the drawing.

## Review the insert and preparation

Identify the metal grade, finish, coating and insert supply arrangement. Surface preparation, bonding system and molding conditions need to be considered together.

## Agree bond verification

Define the test geometry, load direction, conditioning and acceptance criteria. A generic bond-strength number is not a substitute for a representative part test.

## Review a Bonded Part

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
