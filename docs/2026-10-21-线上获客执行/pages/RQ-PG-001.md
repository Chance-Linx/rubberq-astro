## 英文页面稿

# Japanese Formulation. Chinese Precision. Trusted Since 1995.

Precision rubber components, developed around your drawing, material requirements and service conditions. RubberQ brings Japanese formulation support, manufacturing in Fuzhou and in-house testing into one traceable production process.

## Precision starts with the compound

Material selection, mixing and molding work together. We review the polymer family, service medium, temperature, motion and part geometry before agreeing a development route.

## Components made for your assembly

Explore O-rings and seals, custom gaskets, bellows and molded parts. Bring a drawing, a reference sample or an application problem so we can discuss the part and its production requirements.

## From formulation to repeat supply

Compound control, molding, inspection and ERP production records connect the approved sample to later orders. Discuss the inspection plan and documentation your purchasing team requires.

## Applications with demanding operating conditions

Explore semiconductor equipment, liquid cooling and energy storage, EV thermal management and humanoid robotics. Each project starts with its actual operating conditions and validation requirements.

## A manufacturing partner since 1995

Founded as a Sino-Japanese joint venture in 1995, RubberQ operates two facilities in Fuzhou covering approximately 20,000 square metres. Learn about our production, quality systems and laboratory.

## Request a Quote

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
