## 英文页面稿

# Custom Rubber Gaskets

Develop a gasket around the joint it needs to seal. Share the flange or enclosure drawing, fluid exposure, compression limits and assembly method so material and geometry can be reviewed together.

## Start with the joint

Identify the mating surfaces, fasteners, compression stops, allowable gap and installation method. These details shape the gasket section and validation plan.

## Match the compound to the environment

Review fluid contact, temperature cycles, weather exposure and compression recovery. EPDM, FKM, NBR and silicone are candidate material families, selected for the specific application.

## Move from sample to repeat order

Agree dimensions, appearance requirements, inspection points and packaging. For a recurring program, include annual demand and the documentation expected with each batch.

## Request a Gasket Quote

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
