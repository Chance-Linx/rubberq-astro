## 英文页面稿

# Rubber Materials for Precision Components

Compare elastomer families against the medium, temperature, movement and durability requirements of your part. Material selection starts with the application and ends with an agreed compound and validation plan.

## Begin with the operating conditions

List every contacting fluid, its concentration, cleaning exposure, normal and peak temperatures, pressure and motion. Include storage and installation requirements.

## Compare candidate families

Explore FFKM, FKM, EPDM, HNBR, NBR, silicone and other available families. Similar hardness does not mean similar chemical resistance or sealing behaviour.

## Define the compound and acceptance criteria

Use the polymer family to narrow the options, then specify hardness, physical properties, conditioning and test methods for the candidate compound.

## Discuss Material Selection

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
