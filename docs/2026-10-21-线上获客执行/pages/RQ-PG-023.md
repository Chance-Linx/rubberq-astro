## 英文页面稿

# LSR Molding Project Review

Bring a liquid silicone rubber part drawing for a material, tooling and manufacturing review. Critical features, grade requirements and order volume guide the discussion.

## Define the part priorities

Identify thin sections, sealing edges, optical or appearance requirements and permitted parting lines. Share assembly drawings where fit is critical.

## Review the material and production route

Specify hardness, colour, service exposure and any grade-specific requirements. Production arrangements and the process route are confirmed for the project.

## Plan qualification

Agree samples, dimensional checks, functional tests and the documentation needed before production. Tooling and delivery commitments follow the technical review.

## Send an LSR Part Drawing

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
