# Precision Rubber Manufacturing Since 1995

RubberQ was established in Fuzhou in 1995 as a Sino-Japanese joint venture. Our work connects Japanese formulation support with compound preparation, precision molding and in-house testing in China.

## A practical approach to material development

A rubber component has to work in its assembly, its environment and its production process. We start by understanding those requirements and translate them into a compound and manufacturing review.

## Two facilities, a connected process

Our two Fuzhou facilities cover approximately 20,000 square metres. Mixing, molding, inspection and production records support project development and repeat manufacturing.

## Accountability through production

ERP records help connect material batches, production steps and inspection results. For your project, we can discuss the traceability and documentation required for supplier evaluation.

## Discuss Your Project

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
