## 英文页面稿

# Custom Rubber Bellows and Protective Boots

Review flexible bellows and protective boots for industrial mechanisms. Installed length, travel, surrounding media and attachment details define the geometry and material requirements.

## Describe the full movement

Provide compressed and extended dimensions, lateral movement, cycling conditions and available installation space. Include the attachment features at both ends.

## Control exposure and interference

Identify dust, splash, oil, cleaning chemicals and heat sources. Review where folds may rub against adjacent parts or become trapped during movement.

## Check the installed part

Agree a sample review that includes fit, movement, retention and visual inspection. Any cycle-life requirement should include an agreed test method and acceptance criterion.

## Discuss a Bellows Design

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
