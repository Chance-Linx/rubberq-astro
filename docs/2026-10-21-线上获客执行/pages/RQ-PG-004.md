# O-Rings and Custom Rubber Seals

Discuss O-rings and sealing profiles for your assembly. Material, groove dimensions, motion, pressure and service medium guide the review, from a reference size to a custom molded seal.

## Define the sealing interface

Send the seal drawing and, where available, groove and mating-part dimensions. Identify static or dynamic operation, pressure direction, installation method and permissible flash.

## Select a material for the actual exposure

Compare FKM, FFKM, EPDM, HNBR, NBR and silicone families with the fluid, temperature cycle and mechanical requirements. A polymer family alone does not define a qualified compound.

## Agree how the part will be checked

Specify the dimension reference, tolerances, surface requirements and acceptance method. Samples help connect the drawing to the assembled seal before repeat supply.

## Questions about your project

### Can you review a non-standard O-ring?

Send the dimensions or drawing, required quantity and service conditions. We will review the geometry, material and tooling requirements.

### Can an FFKM seal replace an existing seal?

A replacement requires a review of the original specification, geometry and operating conditions, followed by agreed validation. See our FFKM replacement review page.

## Request an O-Ring Quote

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
