## 英文页面稿

# Custom Rubber Parts for Industrial Assemblies

Find a component route for your drawing or application: O-rings and seals, gaskets, bellows, diaphragms, bonded parts and other precision molded components. Share material, quantity and operating requirements for an engineering review.

## O-rings and sealing components

Review cross-section, groove, squeeze and service medium alongside the material. Standard-size references and custom geometries need an agreed inspection basis.

## Gaskets and flexible parts

For gaskets, bellows and diaphragms, the clamping arrangement, movement and assembly interfaces matter as much as the outline drawing.

## Molded and bonded components

Custom shapes and rubber-to-metal parts require attention to tooling, parting lines, inserts, surface preparation and inspection access.
