## 英文页面稿

# Rubber Seal Failure: A Practical Review Checklist

Preserve the failed part and operating history before choosing a replacement. A useful review connects the damage location to assembly, exposure, motion and time in service.

## Record before replacing

Photograph the installed location and damage, keep unused reference parts and record operating changes. Include drawing revision and batch information where available.

## Separate possible causes

Review installation damage, geometry, friction, fluid exposure and temperature rather than assuming every failure is a material problem.

## Test the proposed change

Define what a new sample should demonstrate and how it will be measured. Keep the validation conditions with the decision to change material or geometry.

## Discuss the Specification

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
