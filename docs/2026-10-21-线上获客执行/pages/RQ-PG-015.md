## 英文页面稿

# HNBR Materials for Mechanical Sealing Parts

Evaluate HNBR candidates against the fluid, temperature and repeated mechanical loading of your component. Compound selection and part geometry should be reviewed together.

## Describe the mechanical duty

Provide load direction, motion, cycle conditions and contact surfaces. Include friction, wear and compression requirements where they affect the assembly.

## Identify service exposure

Specify fluids, additives, cleaning conditions and temperature cycles. Review the complete environment with the candidate formulation.

## Make the test representative

Agree hardness, physical-property checks and functional sample testing that reflect the actual component and installation.

## Review a HNBR Application

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
