## 英文页面稿

# Rubber Material Compatibility: What to Specify

Prepare a compatibility review using the exact fluid, concentration, temperature and exposure cycle. Generic family comparisons help narrow a discussion but do not replace compound testing.

## Describe the complete exposure

Include additives, contamination, cleaning and alternating media. A base-fluid name may omit conditions that matter to the compound.

## Define the relevant changes

Agree whether volume, mass, hardness, strength, compression recovery or assembled leakage is being assessed.

## Compare on a common basis

Keep compound identification, temperature, duration and conditioning with the result. Use representative samples for the final application decision.

## Discuss the Specification

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
