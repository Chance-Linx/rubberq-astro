## 英文页面稿

# In-House Rubber Testing and Inspection

Connect material and component requirements to an agreed test plan. RubberQ uses in-house testing and inspection to support compound review, production control and customer evaluation.

## Test the properties that matter

Discuss hardness, tensile behaviour, elongation, aging, compression set and fluid exposure with the conditioning and acceptance requirements of the project.

## Keep conditions with the result

A useful test record identifies the compound, sample, method, temperature, duration and result. Compare data only when the test basis is relevant to the application.

## Review component inspection

Identify dimensions, surface features and assembly checks separately from material testing. Specialised or third-party tests can be discussed where required.

## Discuss a Test Requirement

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
