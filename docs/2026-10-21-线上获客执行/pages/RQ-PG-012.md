# FFKM Materials for Demanding Sealing Conditions

Where service chemistry and temperature challenge other elastomers, FFKM is a candidate family for sealing evaluation. The formulation and actual process conditions determine the testing needed.

## Specify the process exposure

List process gases or liquids, cleaning steps, temperature cycles, vacuum conditions and the seal location. Include any cleanliness or contamination requirements.

## Review a compound, not only a family

Discuss hardness, physical properties and the tests relevant to your equipment. Purity, outgassing and plasma behaviour require formulation-specific evidence.

## Connect selection to the seal

Use the drawing, gland and assembly conditions to plan samples. For equipment maintenance, compare the existing specification before considering a supply change.
