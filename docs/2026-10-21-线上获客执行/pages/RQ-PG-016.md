## 英文页面稿

# NBR Materials for Industrial Rubber Parts

Review NBR candidates for a defined industrial application. Fluid composition, temperature and the required balance of flexibility and mechanical properties guide compound selection.

## Define the oil or fluid exposure

Identify the contacting medium and any cleaning or storage exposure. Include temperature, pressure and seal motion.

## Specify mechanical requirements

List hardness, deformation limits, wear concerns and dimensions that control assembly function.

## Compare before changing materials

When replacing an existing compound, share its designation and failure history. Agree a sample and validation plan for the alternative.

## Review a NBR Application

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
