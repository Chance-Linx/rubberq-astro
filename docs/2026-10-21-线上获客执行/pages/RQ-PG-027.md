# Quality Control and Production Traceability

Define quality requirements from material preparation to finished-part inspection. RubberQ combines in-house testing with ERP production records to support samples, repeat orders and supplier evaluation.

## Agree the acceptance plan

Start with the drawing revision, material specification and critical characteristics. Discuss inspection frequency, reporting and customer-specific requirements.

## Connect records through production

Material batch identification, process records and inspection results support traceability. Discuss which records and documents need to accompany your project.

## Review our management systems

RubberQ holds IATF 16949:2016, ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015 certifications. Request the current certificates and applicable scope for your supplier evaluation.

## Request Supplier Information

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
