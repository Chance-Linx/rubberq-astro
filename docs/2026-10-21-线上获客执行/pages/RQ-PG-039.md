# Rubber Hardness: Specifying a Useful Requirement

Hardness is one part of a rubber specification. Include the scale, method, tolerance and measurement conditions with the functional requirements of the component.

## State the scale and method

A hardness value needs its scale and test basis. Sample thickness, conditioning and measurement approach influence how results can be compared.

## Connect hardness to function

Sealing, flexibility and assembly behaviour also depend on geometry and other material properties. Hardness alone does not describe compression recovery or chemical resistance.

## Review samples in the assembly

Use functional checks and dimensional inspection alongside the hardness requirement. Avoid treating cross-scale conversions as acceptance measurements.
