## 英文页面稿

# Custom Rubber Compound Supply

Discuss compound supply for your molding requirements. Share the material specification, receiving process, required form and volume so we can review technical fit and a supply arrangement.

## Specify the compound and receiving process

Provide the polymer family or existing designation, hardness, physical-property requirements, cure system where specified and the intended molding process.

## Agree handling and release requirements

Discuss packaging form, storage, shelf-life documentation, batch identification and release testing. Required quantities and shipment conditions are confirmed for the project.

## Validate in your process

Agree trial material and evaluation criteria with the receiving production team. A compound must be assessed against the actual process and finished-part requirements.

## Clarify development and supply responsibilities

Identify who owns the specification and how changes will be controlled. Commercial, formulation confidentiality and development arrangements are agreed project by project.

## Request a Compound Supply Review

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
