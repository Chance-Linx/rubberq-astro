# Request a Quote for Multiple Rubber Parts

Submit a group of rubber components for review. Keep each part number connected to its drawing revision, material specification and expected quantity.

## Organise the part list

Provide part number, drawing revision, material, unit of measure and quantity for each item. Identify common materials or shared assembly requirements.

## Clarify commercial scope

Indicate sample quantities, recurring demand, tooling ownership requirements and delivery destination.

## Define approval requirements

Include inspection, traceability, testing and documentation needs. This helps separate tooling, development and production discussions.

## Submit a Multi-Part RFQ

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
