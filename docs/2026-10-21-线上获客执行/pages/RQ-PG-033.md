## 英文页面稿

# Rubber Components for Humanoid Robotics

Review flexible protection, contact surfaces and bonded components around the movement and loading of your mechanism. Share the drawing and functional requirements for an engineering discussion.

## Describe movement and contact

Identify travel, rotation, repeated loading, contact surfaces and available installation space. Include dust, fluid and cleaning exposure.

## Match geometry and material

Bellows, pads and bonded parts need different material and design reviews. Discuss friction, deformation, retention and the assembly interfaces that control function.

## Validate the mechanical duty

Define a representative sample test with the loading, motion and environmental conditions. Endurance or dynamic-performance targets need their own measurement basis.

## Discuss Your Application

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
