# Rubber Engineering Resources

Find material comparisons, drawing checklists and testing questions for your rubber component project. Use the guidance to prepare a specification and identify what needs application-specific validation.

Compare polymer families, service media and the questions needed to narrow a compound choice.

## Drawing and design

Review O-ring dimensions, gasket joints, molding considerations and the information that helps an engineering team evaluate a part.

## Testing and troubleshooting

Connect a failure observation to the loading, exposure and test conditions. A useful investigation preserves the evidence from the actual component.
