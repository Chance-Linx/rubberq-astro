# ACM Material Selection for Rubber Components

Assess ACM as a candidate material against the specific fluid, temperature and mechanical requirements of your part. Share the existing specification or application data for review.

## Provide the material specification

Include the current designation, physical-property requirements and the test methods used for acceptance.

## Describe the exposure cycle

Supply the fluids, temperature cycle, movement and storage requirements. Review changes in flexibility and sealing behaviour that matter to the component.

## Connect the material to a sample

Agree a compound and inspection plan for the actual geometry rather than relying only on generic family descriptions.

## Review a ACM Application

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
