## 英文页面稿

# Rubber Compounding and Formulation Support

Compounded by Japan. Built in China. Tested in-House. Connect your service conditions to a candidate compound, controlled preparation and an agreed release-test plan.

## Translate the application into a specification

Share the service medium, temperature, motion, target properties and processing requirements. Include an existing material specification where available.

## Control the preparation route

Recipe, weighing, mixing sequence and batch identification help connect development work to repeat preparation. Discuss the controls and documentation your project needs.

## Agree batch release

Review the tests relevant to the material, such as cure behaviour, viscosity, hardness and physical properties. Test selection and limits are agreed for the compound.

## Compound supply or finished parts

Discuss whether the project needs a compound supply review, a molded component or both. For external compound supply, include the receiving process and technical specification.

## Discuss a Compound Requirement

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
