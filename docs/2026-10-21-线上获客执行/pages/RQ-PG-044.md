Send your drawing or describe the application. Include material requirements, service conditions and quantity so we can review the next technical and commercial steps.

## Drawing or reference part

Include the drawing revision, critical dimensions and assembly interfaces. If you do not have a drawing, describe the part function and available reference information.

## Material and service conditions

List the medium, temperature, pressure or movement and any existing material specification. Note the tests and documentation required.

## Quantity and project stage

Provide sample quantity, expected repeat demand and your project stage. Tooling, qualification and delivery requirements are discussed after review.

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
