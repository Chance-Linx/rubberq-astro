Share the part, material and evaluation requirements for your sample request. We will review what needs to be defined before samples can be prepared.

Provide the drawing, existing designation or reference part details, required quantity and material requirements.

## Describe the evaluation

Tell us how the sample will be checked, the assembly conditions and the acceptance criteria.

Sample availability, tooling, charges and timing depend on the project and are discussed after the technical review.

## Submit Sample Requirements

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
