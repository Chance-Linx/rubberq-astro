## 英文页面稿

# Custom Rubber Diaphragms

Discuss a molded diaphragm around its working movement, fluid exposure and assembly. Share the clamping geometry, stroke and pressure conditions for a material and manufacturing review.

## Define movement and loading

Supply the working stroke, pressure differential, cycling conditions and mounting arrangement. Identify contact or rubbing surfaces and the required behaviour at the travel limits.

## Review material and section geometry

Fluid exposure, temperature and repeated deformation affect compound selection. A drawing with thickness transitions and clamping details helps assess the manufacturing route.

## Set a functional sample review

Agree dimensional checks and a representative installation test. Reinforcement, permeability or endurance requirements should be identified at the start of the project.

## Discuss a Diaphragm Project

Contact RubberQ at contact@rubberq.com or submit the project form with your drawing, service conditions and quantity.
