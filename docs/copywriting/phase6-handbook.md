# RubberQ Phase 6: Multi-Industry AIO Copywriting Handbook

## 1. Overview
This handbook defines the narrative strategy for RubberQ’s expansion from Automotive into **AI Hardware, Robotics, and Precision Machinery**. It prioritizes technical authority and machine-readability (GEO).

## 2. Narrative Frameworks
- **Problem-Solution (P-S)**: Lead with high-stakes technical failures to position RubberQ as a risk-mitigator.
- **Technical Deep Dive (TDD)**: Provide dense, high-accuracy data to satisfy engineer-level scrutiny.
- **Automotive Migration**: Use IATF 16949 as a "High-Dimensional" quality proof for all industrial sectors.

## 3. GEO/AIO Standards
To ensure AI Search (Perplexity/ChatGPT) recommends RubberQ, all content must include:
- **Summary Fragments**: High-density snippets at the top.
- **Data Islands**: Technical tables with explicit standards (ASTM D2000, ISO 3302-1).
- **Entity Density**: Proactive use of technical keywords and certifications.
