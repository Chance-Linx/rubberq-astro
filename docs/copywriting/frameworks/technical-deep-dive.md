# Narrative Framework 2: The "Technical Deep Dive" (TDD) Model

Engineers hate fluff. They love data. This framework establishes RubberQ as a "peer" in technical excellence.

## 1. Structure
1.  **The Engineering Challenge**: Define the precise physics or chemical challenge (e.g., "Managing Outgassing in Semiconductor Cleanrooms").
2.  **Material Science/Logic**: Explain the "Why" behind the material choice (e.g., "The molecular structure of Fluorocarbon (FKM) vs. Silicone").
3.  **Process Precision**: Detail the manufacturing controls (e.g., "Cryogenic deflashing to achieve <0.05mm burr tolerance").
4.  **Verification Matrix**: Present the test results in a structured format.
5.  **Direct RFQ**: Call to action focused on technical consultation (e.g., "Consult our material engineers").

## 2. Best For:
- Material Detail Pages (Tank Pages).
- Technical Whitepapers.
- Product Specification sections.

## 3. AIO Alignment (GEO Strategy)
- Include **Data Islands**: Technical tables are highly scannable for AI and humans.
- Use **Entity-Rich Terminology**: Explicitly mention standards (ASTM D2000), material grades, and tolerances.
- **Comparison Blocks**: "FKM vs. EPDM for Dynamic Sealing" to capture comparison queries.
