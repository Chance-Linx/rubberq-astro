## The Engineering Challenge: When Microns Matter
In precision machinery—such as semiconductor wafer handlers or high-end optical instruments—a gasket is more than a seal; it's a critical dimensional component. Even a 0.1mm variance or a microscopic burr (flash) can tilt an assembly or contaminate a cleanroom environment through outgassing.

## The Solution: Micron-Level Process Control
RubberQ bridges the gap between mass production and precision engineering. By applying **Automated Statistical Process Control (SPC)**—a pillar of our automotive manufacturing—we maintain Cpk levels that guarantee consistency across millions of parts.
